The term "Demon" is used to refer to a variety of races that all share the same infernal-like traits, this, however, this term is improper, as not all races that share these traits are **true demons**, and they are called **improper demons**.
# List of true demon races
True demons are defined as the product of a [fallen angel](Angel.md) and a [mortal race](List%20of%20Mortal%20Races), they are more often than not characterised by conflicting traits in their body, such as having wings, but a body not made for flight.

# List of improper demons 
Improper demons are defined as any race that has been classified as a demon, but isn't in any way related to fallen angels and/or mortal races
