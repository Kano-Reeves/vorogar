The scribe calendar is one of the three common calendar systems in use, it is used exclusively by the [[Order of the Scribes]], it was created to record history as precisely and efficiently as possible.
# Structure
The scribe calendar is divided in 10 months of 50 days each, which are usually just numbered, the months divided in 5 weeks of 10 days each, unlike the other calendars in use, this calendar never resets.
# Usage
This calendar is only used by the Order of the Scribes
