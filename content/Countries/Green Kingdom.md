The Green Kingdom (official name: Frai Friod), also at times called Coalition of the Green, is a country found in the [[Central Continent]], sharing borders with the [[Elven Federation]] at east and the [[Winged Republic]] on the south, it also neighbours the [[Everlasting Forest]] on the south-east, between the two other borders.
# History
The Green Kingdom was founded around year 20787, by founders [[Sfaom]] and [[Veghava]], with the intent of demonstrating that goblins and orcs could live civilised like the other races, and to have a zero racial discrimination policy against all races, for that reason they declared themselves neutral during the [[Demon-Human Wars]].
# Population
As of 20943 the Green Kingdom has a population of over 296 million people, mostly [goblins](Goblin) and [orcs](Orc), but it also counts some major minorities of [hags](Hag).

The official languages are [goblinish](Goblinish%20Language.md) and [orcish](Orcish%20Language.md)
# Government
The Green Kingdom's central government is formed by a parliament, which is a council of different races, the percentage of seats assigned to each race is proportional to the percentage of that race in the population, the majority of the seats belong to orcs and goblins.

Each race represented in the parliament also has a spokesperson called a representative, without having one central monarch or president, instead, when the need of representing the country comes, the representatives discuss among themselves to send the most fit for the job.
# Culture
Being one of the most diverse of countries, the Green Kingdom has a mix of goblinish and orcish traditions
