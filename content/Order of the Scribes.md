The Order of the Scribes, at times also called Chronos' scribes, is a secret order instituted by [[Chronos]] to record all the history throughtout all planets and [planes of existance](Plane%20of%20Existance).
# Origin
Any instance of the Order is instituted as soon as written history starts, Chronos will give the duty to record to a person or a small group of people, the order will then naturally expand as the population of the planet rises.

All the orders use the same calendar, the [[Scribe Calendar]], with year 0 being the start of the order.
## Vorogar's Order
[[Vorogar]]'s order counts, as of year 20943, a little over 1,5 million members among scribes, scouts, archivists and oracles dispatched on the entire planet.

Vorogar's Central library is located in the [[Central Continent]] for ease of access
# Roles
To make work more organised, and easier on everyone, the Order commonly assigns different roles to different members, these roles are, in order of how the work is done:
- **Scout**: gather informations about the events happening as they happen
- **Oracles**: confirm and add detail to what happened, and signal the possibility of a next big event
- **Scribes:** based on the notes from scouts and oracles, write the official document
- **Archivist**: in charge of organising and archiving all the official documents written
Especially in smaller and less inhabited parts of the world, a single person may cover more than one role
