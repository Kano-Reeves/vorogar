Humans are a [mortal race](List%20of%20Mortal%20Races.md), widely considered the default race of [[Vorogar]], for their lack of distinguishing features, as such, their lifespan is considered the default one, and is used to determine whether a race is long or short lived, and whether their features are distinguishing or not.
# Physiology
## Development
The only actual trait that distinguishes human development from development of other races is that, unlike most species, humans aren't born with their adult teeth, being born with so-called "milk teeth" that fall out around the 6 to 10 years of age, and get replaced with adult teeth.
## Half humans
As any other [humanoid race](List%20of%20Humanoid%20Races.md), humans can breed with any other humanoid race, all that seems to do to the offspring is dilute the racial traits of the other parent.
## Tiefling humans
[[Tiefling]] humans have tinted skin the color of the tiefling origin, a thick tail and horns, that vary in shape and size.
# Spread
Humans are mostly found in the north eastern part of the [[Central Continent]]
